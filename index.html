DATABASE ARCHITECTURE

Neon PostgreSQL Setup

PR Real Estate is engineered with full support for Neon Serverless PostgreSQL.

Exactly Where to Paste Your Neon Link:

  1. Open the file .env in the project root folder.
  2. Locate the line: DATABASE_URL=
  3. Paste your Neon link directly after the equals sign, for example:
DATABASE_URL=postgresql://neondb_owner:YOUR_PASSWORD@ep-cool-fog-123456.us-east-2.aws.neon.tech/neondb?sslmode=require
